Shipping & Care FAQ

Does freeze-dried candy melt during hot weather transit?

Unlike regular chewy candy, freeze-dried treats have almost zero moisture content! They won't melt into a sticky puddle during transit, though we recommend keeping them out of direct sunlight.

How long do freeze-dried candies stay fresh?

Our heavy-duty, heat-sealed bags keep moisture out. Unopened bags stay crisp and fresh for months. Once opened, seal the zip-top tight to keep humidity from softening the texture.

Why are Caramel Pops marked as local pickup only?

Our freeze-dried Caramel Pops expand into extremely delicate, giant puffs that can easily crush under postal conveyor belts, so we reserve them exclusively for local pickup to guarantee quality.

How do I pay for my shipped order?

After submitting your order and delivery address, our checkout provides an instant direct link to complete payment through Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal using your generated Order ID.
